Responsible use

Acceptable use policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Casta Web Doctor provides basic diagnostic checks for public websites. By using it, you agree to use it lawfully, responsibly, and only for legitimate testing.

Allowed use

  • Scan websites you own or are explicitly authorized to assess.
  • Use results for basic troubleshooting and learning.
  • Keep scan frequency reasonable and respect the target website's terms and availability.

Prohibited use

  • Do not scan a website without permission when authorization is required.
  • Do not use the service to overload, disrupt, harass, or degrade a website or service.
  • Do not attempt to reach private networks, localhost, cloud metadata services, or other internal resources.
  • Do not submit secrets, authentication tokens, personal data, or confidential URLs.
  • Do not bypass rate limits or other safeguards.

Limitations

The tool performs a limited set of checks and is not a penetration-testing platform, a complete security audit, or a guarantee that a website is safe. Results may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Enforcement and changes

The operator may restrict access or change the service to protect the service, target websites, and other users. This policy may be updated as the project changes.

This policy is a practical usage statement, not legal advice. The operator should review it against applicable law and the deployed service before public launch.
