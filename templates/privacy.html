Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This notice explains what happens when you use Casta Web Doctor, a website diagnostic tool. The operator should review it against the actual hosting and logging configuration before treating it as a final legal statement.

Please do not submit confidential URLs. A URL may contain private information in its path or query string. Scan only public websites you own or have permission to assess.

Information you submit

When you request a scan, the service receives the website address you enter and makes an HTTP/HTTPS request to that public destination. It processes the response status, response time, final redirect destination, HTTPS status, and selected response headers to return the scan report. The tool does not require an account.

Storage and operational logs

The application code does not intentionally save scan reports to an application database. However, the hosting provider and infrastructure may process operational data such as request metadata, IP addresses, error events, or service logs. The exact fields and retention period depend on the deployed hosting and logging settings and must be verified by the operator. Until those settings have been checked and documented, no fixed retention period is promised here.

How information is used

Submitted addresses and website responses are processed to provide the scan you request, operate and troubleshoot the service, and protect it from abuse. Do not use the scanner to submit credentials, private internal addresses, or personal information.

Third-party services

The repository contains a Render deployment configuration, but the live host and its current logging configuration must be confirmed by the operator. If the service is hosted by a third party, that provider may process connection and operational data under its own terms and privacy documentation.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to submit a URL. For privacy questions or requests, contact the project operator through the contact method published in the project repository. A direct support contact should be added here before the service is promoted to a broad public audience.

Changes

This notice may change as the service and its hosting configuration change. The operator should update the date and content whenever data collection, storage, processors, or retention practices change.

This notice is informational and is not legal advice. The operator must verify actual data practices and applicable legal obligations before public launch.
