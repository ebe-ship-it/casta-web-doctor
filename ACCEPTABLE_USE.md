# Acceptable use policy

**Status:** Draft policy for an early beta. This is a service-use policy, not legal advice.

## Use only with permission

You may scan a website only if you own it or have explicit permission from its owner or authorized operator. A website being publicly reachable does not automatically mean its operator has consented to security testing.

## Prohibited use

Do not use Casta Web Doctor to:
- access accounts, systems, data, or services without authorization;
- bypass authentication, access controls, rate limits, or other safeguards;
- disrupt a service, overload a server, or interfere with another person's use of a system;
- test internal, private, loopback, link-local, or cloud metadata destinations;
- collect personal data, secrets, credentials, or confidential information;
- harass, threaten, defraud, impersonate, or violate another person's rights;
- break applicable laws, court orders, or third-party terms.

## Scope and limits

The tool performs limited, low-volume checks of a submitted public website. It is not a penetration-testing platform, vulnerability scanner, legal compliance certification, or substitute for a qualified security assessment. A scan result may be incomplete or inaccurate; a successful scan does not mean a site is secure.

## Abuse and service protection

The service may reject requests, impose rate limits, suspend scanning, or preserve relevant operational records where reasonably necessary to protect the service and investigate suspected abuse, subject to applicable law and the published privacy notice.

## Legal compliance

Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission and that their activity complies with applicable law. The service operator must also review obligations that apply to operating the service, including privacy, data protection, consumer-facing disclosures, and hosting-provider requirements. This policy does not guarantee that use or operation of the service is lawful in every jurisdiction.

## Reporting concerns

Report suspected misuse or security issues privately through a verified contact method published by the service maintainer. Do not post credentials, private URLs, personal data, or exploit details publicly.
